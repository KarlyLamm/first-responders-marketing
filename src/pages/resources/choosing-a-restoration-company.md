## Questions to ask

1. **Do you answer 24/7, and how fast can you be here?** Water damage gets worse by the hour. Ask for a realistic arrival time.
2. **Where are you based?** A local shop with a real address is easier to hold accountable than a storm chaser who leaves town.
3. **Will you document the loss for my insurer?** Look for photos, moisture logs and itemized estimates.
4. **Do you also rebuild?** Companies that only dry out leave you hiring a second contractor.
5. **Do you do roofs too?** After storms, the roof and the interior are often one claim. One company keeps it simple.
6. **What certifications do you hold?** Ask about restoration training and roofing manufacturer certifications, and check them.
7. **Can I see recent reviews?** Check Google, BBB and Angi, not only testimonials on the company's site.

## Red flags

- Pressure to sign on the spot before you have talked to your insurer
- Asking you to pay the full deductible up front or offering to waive it
- No local address or a phone number that only goes to voicemail at night
